# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

**Primary: working musicians on Lower Broadway in Nashville** — sidemen, subs and bandleaders.
They pull up a song's Nashville Number System chart on a phone in the middle of a set:
dark stage, loud room, one hand free, a few seconds to find it and read it at arm's length.

**Secondary: the people who keep the charts right** — the owner, the co-owner and reviewers who
check AI-read charts against the scanned originals, usually on a laptop.

## Product Purpose

Free, open-source number charts for the songs played on Broadway. The charts are read from about
1,900 real handwritten Broadway charts (one scanned PDF per song). Any chart can be shown in any
key, as numbers or chord names, and works offline once saved to the phone.

Success is a player finding the right chart and reading it on stage without missing a beat.

## Positioning

The charts come from the working players' own handwritten book, corrected by the community
(a change goes live after 3 approvals) and marked Verified by admins. It is built for the stage,
not the practice room.

## Operating Context

- Stage use: phone in hand or on a stand, dark room, loud band, quick song changes, setlists,
  one-tap next song, stage mode with autoscroll and a beat light.
- Review use: split screen with the scanned PDF beside the chart editor, keyboard shortcuts.
- Anyone can view without signing in. Signing in (email magic link) is needed to suggest edits or vote.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No lyrics stored, ever. Charts hold numbers, structure, dynamics and short cue words
  (6 words or fewer).
- Code is MIT. Chart data contributed by users is CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Has to work offline: charts are cached on the phone.
- An installable phone web app, not a native app.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Broadway Book.
- Voice: plain and short, like a bandleader calling the next tune
  (current copy: "Flag for me", "Just for me", "Suggest for everyone").

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor.
- Chart text aims for 7:1 contrast.
- Tap targets are 44px or larger.
